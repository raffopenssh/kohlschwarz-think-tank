5. **mit-solve**: Global Challenges are confirmed to launch in Sep 2026 with early-stage eligibility; the 2026 "10 for 10" ($100k) was for at-scale solutions only and closed 2026-05-21.
6. **public-io** is a consultancy, not a funder — drop.
7. **cdl-climate**: Climate stream is hosted at CDL-Paris and CDL-Vancouver (not UK/Berlin); 2026/27 applications were open at time of check, sessions Oct–Jun.
8. **aws-activate**: bootstrapped tier is $1k (up to $5k), not $1k–200k for self-applicants; $200k needs an Activate Provider org ID.
