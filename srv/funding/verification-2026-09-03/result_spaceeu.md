1. **cassini-accelerator**: deadline 2026-10-02 is correct (T&C updated 29 Jul 2026), but eligibility is stricter than noted: SME legal entity + growth stage with sales expected → not eligible now.
2. **nasa-space-apps**: 2026 event is **14–15 Nov 2026**, not early October. Vienna local in-person event confirmed and accepting participants.
3. **horizon-euspa-2027**: topic ID and deadline in notes are wrong. Real topic: HORIZON-2027-EUSPA-SPACE-51, opens 2026-10-14, deadline 2027-02-17, only €0.98m / 1 project, preparedness-focused with mandatory practitioner partners.
4. **ucpm**: 2026 KAPP deadline was 21 May 2026 (not 19 May).
5. **esa-bic-austria**: official open-call page lists only Styria or Lower Austria as required location (not Upper Austria/Carinthia); Salzburg via local partner. Natural persons eligible – confirmed. Next cut-off currently "to be announced".
6. **esa-bass-kickstart**: not "continuous thematic calls" for the open call – open call has fixed batch cut-offs; the next is 30 Oct 2026. Programme rebranded under ESA ACCESS.
7. **esa-osip**: contracting needs an ESA-eligible entity (esa-star registration) – a registered sole trader may suffice; not for pure private individuals.
8. **eic-accelerator**: cut-offs are bimonthly in 2026 (Jan 7, Mar 4, May 6, Jul 8, Sep 2, Nov 4); natural persons "willing to set up an SME" are formally eligible.
9. **ffg-asap**: no 2026 call has been published; FFG site blocks automated access – verify manually at https://www.ffg.at/ausschreibungen or via FFG newsletter. Amount note correct for 2025.
10. **eit-climaccelerator**: 2026 edition themed "AI & Deep-tech for Climate", organiser call only; no Austrian programme; not a direct grant.
11. **life-ngo-consortia / life-2026**: 2026 SAP deadline is 22 Sep 2026 (not "Sep–Oct"); strategic projects concept note 3 Sep 2026 / full 4 Mar 2027.
12. **digital-europe-genai-pa**: closed 3 Mar 2026 with 56 proposals; no 2027 GenAI-PA topic announced.
